Meet Medi, your AI family health assistant

Describe how you feel in your own words. Medi checks for warning signs first, asks a few focused questions, and helps you understand what might be going on and what level of care makes sense. It supports your family's healthcare; it doesn't replace your doctor.

🩺

Focused questions

Medi asks only what matters: when it started, how it's changing, how severe it is, and what else you've noticed.

🚦

Right level of care

One of four clear answers: emergency (call 911), urgent care today, a normal clinic visit, or home care.

🔍

Honest possibilities

Possible causes with what supports or weakens each one and what's still unknown. No made-up percentages.

📷

Photo check

Snap a rash, wound, or swelling and Medi will factor what it sees into its assessment.

🚨

Emergency safety net

Warning signs like chest pain or stroke symptoms trigger an instant call-for-help alert.

📄

Visit summary

Print a doctor-style summary with questions to bring to your real appointment.
